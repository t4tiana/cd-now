# CDnow company evidence available by July 15, 1998

**Research cutoff: July 15, 1998.** This is a source inventory for the investment assessment, not a reconstruction using later filings. The figures below describe CDnow as a whole. They do not measure the supplied Q1 1997 cohort's acquisition cost, gross margin, cash contribution, or revenue eligible for the proposed 50% repayment share.

## IPO disclosures

I reviewed supplied copies of CDnow's SEC [February 9, 1998 prospectus (424B1)](../CDNOW_data/SEC_424B1.txt) and [February 3 S-1 amendment](../CDNOW_data/SEC_S1_A.txt). The 424B1 SEC header records a February 11 filing date; its prospectus is dated February 9. Both precede the decision date. The final prospectus is the primary source for the 1997 figures below; the amendment corroborates the annual statements. I inspected the supplied files locally. Direct retrieval from the SEC archive was unavailable in this environment, so their local text has not been matched against a live SEC copy. The filings are CDnow's own disclosures, not independent verification of management estimates.

## Gaps that public research has not closed

1. **Later-period direct financials and comparability.** The February prospectus now supplies the 1997 statements and accounting definitions directly. It does not give this cohort's costs or Q1 1998 accounting statements. Obtain period-consistent Q1 1998 financials and explain any later accounting reclassifications before comparing gross margins. The May registration amount must not be treated as actual proceeds.
2. **Comparable acquisition cohorts and channel spend.** None of these reports states what CDnow spent to acquire the supplied Q1 1997 cohort or a proposed 1998 quarterly cohort, how many new purchasers each channel delivered, or whether older cohorts' repeat buying resembled the supplied file. Request quarterly first-purchase cohorts, spend and channel allocation, and comparable repeat revenue. This could change the future-cohort forecast and advance size.
3. **Contribution and repayment base.** The public figures do not break out CD procurement, fulfillment, shipping subsidies, payment fees, returns/refunds, support, or collected versus recorded revenue by cohort. Request these and the rules for assigning sales and repayments to a financed cohort. This could change both CDnow's ability to bear a 50% revenue share and the investor's time to the 1.2× cap.
4. **Cash commitments and near-term liquidity.** The reported March 31 cash balance is a point in time, and the proposed follow-on offering was reportedly canceled. Request a current balance, committed alliance/advertising payment schedule, working-capital needs, and confirmation of the offering's status. This could change the assessment of counterparty and collection risk. We have not verified public Q2 1998 results as published by the decision date, so do not use them.

**Research boundary:** Large-scale changes in how media is distributed, shared, or consumed are outside this historical assessment. No post–July 15, 1998 substantive reporting was used to infer what investors knew then.
