# Late-July CDnow 10-Q: a decision-timing illustration

**Decision date remains July 15, 1998.** This document was filed after that date. It supports only the separately labeled retrospective afterword in the memo, never in the July 15 evidence set, forecast, advance calculation, or recommendation.

## Source and provenance

I reviewed a supplied copy of [CDnow, Inc., Form 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 1998](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050372/000103605098001219/0001036050-98-001219.txt), accession **0001036050-98-001219**. The SEC header says **“FILED AS OF DATE: 19980728”**; the SEC document marker reads **19980729**. CDnow is the reporting company; the statements are labeled **unaudited**. I inspected the supplied copy because direct SEC retrieval was blocked, so the contents have not been independently matched to the live SEC page. The public availability date should not be inferred from the June 30 period end. It was after July 15 under either late-July date.

This is a quarterly report, not evidence that CDnow issued additional shares on July 28. The cover reports **16,266,397 common shares outstanding as of July 24, 1998** (cover, p. 1). Part II, Item 2 (pp. 14–15) describes the **February 13 IPO**, completed with the underwriters' option on **March 4**: CDnow sold **4,561,250** shares at $16 and selling shareholders sold another **153,750**; only CDnow's sale produced company proceeds. It also describes a **May 18 warrant**, exercisable for **226,892** shares, granted as part of a strategic alliance. A warrant is a right to buy shares later, not evidence those shares were sold on May 18 or July 28. The filing does not identify a July 28 equity sale or its proceeds. The increase from **7,845,684** common shares at December 31, 1997 to **16,266,397** at June 30, 1998 (balance sheet, p. 1) spans several earlier transactions and must not be attributed wholesale to July 28.

The filing itself reports **$67,167,862** of net IPO proceeds in Part II, Item 2 (p. 14), while its cash-flow statement and IPO note report **$67,077,862** (pp. 3–4). The $90,000 difference is unresolved in this copy. Neither figure represents a July financing.

## Use in the separated memo afterword

The memo presents the **July 15** recommendation first, then its separated afterword states that CDnow filed Q2 results in late July. They showed further sales growth but a large operating cash outflow, a $47.3 million June cash balance, and substantial alliance commitments. A committee meeting **after** that filing would revisit CDnow's payment capacity and the proposed advance. It would still need cohort-level acquisition costs, contribution, revenue attribution, and collection terms. The late-July figures must not be used to revise the fictional July 15 forecast or imply we foresaw the filing.

This filing does not resolve the status of the **May proposed follow-on offering**, reported canceled in June. It reports no cash proceeds from such an offering in the first-half cash-flow statement. The February IPO proceeds and the May alliance warrant are distinct transactions.
