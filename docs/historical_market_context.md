# Historical market context for the CDnow assessment

Assume an investment memo dated **July 15, 1998**. Use external information only when its original publication date is on or before that date; an archive's upload date is not its original publication date. These sources give context, not an explanation established by the transaction file. The latest source used below is dated May 22, 1998.

- The Recording Industry Association of America's 1997 year-end figures, released in February 1998, showed a **6.5% decline in total domestic audio and video unit shipments** but a **2.3% increase in full-length CD unit shipments to retail**. The measures are manufacturer shipments, not CDnow sales or customer-level demand. The mixed picture does not establish a broad CD retail collapse that explains the Q1 1997 cohort's later activity. [Contemporaneous report of the RIAA release](https://www.stereophile.com/content/riaa-releases-1997-year-end-music-sales-statistics).
- February 1998 reporting described CDnow as operating since **1994** and identified growing competition in online music retail, including Music Boulevard and distribution partnerships. This rules out a 1997 company launch as the reason every customer appears in Q1 1997, but competition is only a **possible** contributor to later behavior, not a cause identified by this dataset. [Contemporaneous CDnow reporting](https://www.wired.com/1998/02/cdnow-goes-to-market-2/).
- CDnow reported **$10 million of company-wide revenue in Q1 1998**, compared with **$2.6 million in Q1 1997**, and said it added **135,000 new customers** in Q1 1998, reaching **432,000 cumulative purchasing customers**. It also reported a **$9.2 million Q1 1998 net loss**. These figures were reported in the [May 16, 1998 issue of *Billboard*](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-All-Music/Billboard/90s/1998/BB-1998-05-16.pdf); a [May 3, 1998 report](https://www.stereophile.com/content/cdnow-tops-chart-expands-europe) independently relayed the $10 million revenue and customer counts. The sales growth means this one Q1 1997 cohort's declining calendar activity must not be described as a CDnow-wide decline. The net loss adds company-level financing risk, but it does not reveal this cohort's contribution or repayment capacity.
- A [May 22, 1998 interview in *Radio & Records*](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-All-Music/Archive-RandR/1990s/1998/RR-1998-05-22.pdf) reported that **51% of CDnow's Q1 1998 sales came from existing customers** and that **1997 company-wide advertising outlays were $6.8 million, versus $61,000 in 1996**. This is evidence that repeat sales and advertising mattered at the company level. The article does not define the sales attribution rule or allocate advertising to the supplied Q1 1997 cohort; neither number establishes this cohort's repeat revenue or acquisition cost.

## How to pair the sources with cohort findings

| Observed cohort question | Contemporary context | Limit on the inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why is Q1 1997 larger than later quarters? | CDnow had operated since 1994, and company advertising rose in 1997. | Every supplied customer had to make their first recorded purchase in Q1 1997 by definition. Company-wide advertising cannot prove a Q1 campaign caused this cohort's entry. |
| Does falling activity in this cohort mean CDnow itself was shrinking? | CDnow reported higher Q1 1998 company-wide revenue than Q1 1997 and 135,000 new customers in Q1 1998. | Other customers and cohorts are outside the supplied transaction file; company growth does not prove the economics of this cohort or future cohorts. |
| Does company-wide growth establish that a cohort advance is safe? | CDnow reported a $9.2 million Q1 1998 net loss alongside $10 million of revenue. | A company net loss does not measure this cohort's contribution or cash available for repayments; cohort costs, cash timing, and collection terms are still needed. |
| Was there an industry-wide fall in demand for retail CDs? | Total recorded-music shipments fell in 1997, while full-length CD shipments to retail rose. | Manufacturer shipments and this cohort's online purchases are different measures. Neither identifies why individual customers returned or did not return. |

The supplied file intentionally selects people whose first-ever CDnow purchase was in Q1 1997; their presence in that quarter is guaranteed by the cohort definition. Compare like-aged activity and later-date repeat buying before interpreting the decline in calendar-quarter purchasing customers. CDnow acquisition spending by cohort and channel, variable costs, pricing, service experience, and comparable cohort histories are not in the file or these public reports. Do not use later market outcomes to explain or judge the July 1998 decision.

Do not use CDnow's full Q2 1998 company results unless their **publication date** can be verified as July 15, 1998 or earlier. A June 30 financial period end alone does not establish that investors could already read the results.
